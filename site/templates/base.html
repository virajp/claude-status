Every internal link in a TEMPLATE goes through `get_url(path="@/...md")`. That form resolves against the content library, so a link to a page that does not exist fails `zola build` outright. A hand-written `href="/x/"` does not — `zola check` does not read template hrefs at all, and neither does the build. If you write a raw internal href here, nothing will ever tell you when it rots. 2. Every internal link in CONTENT is written `@/page.md`, which `zola check` does validate. Root-relative and `../` links are silently unchecked.
