## The one that matters

PROBLEM 2026-08-21 ★5 | git/budget |
`e2e::a_hanging_git_costs_one_shared_budget_not_one_per_subprocess` never
measured the git budget. macOS assesses a **newly written** executable on first
execution — measured **466 ms** fresh against ~4 ms warm — before the
interpreter reads a line. The test wrote a fresh shim into a fresh `TempDir`
every run, so under the 250 ms shared budget the deadline fired *during the
assessment* and the child died having done nothing: no output, no marker, never
reaching `sleep 30`. It was measuring one Gatekeeper stall — unbounded and
load-sensitive — which is why it was the only test in the suite that ever flaked
(948 ms against a 900 ms bound). Fixed with a `--warm` invocation outside the
timed section plus an `invocations >= 2` assertion that makes it fail rather
than pass vacuously; proven by removing the warm-up and watching it fail.

PROBLEM 2026-08-21 ★4 | self/diagnosis | **I got this wrong first and wrote the
wrong conclusion into the plan doc** — claimed `run_bounded` could not exec a
PATH-resolved script. Spawn always succeeded. What made it diagnosable was
narrating the spawn error: `.ok()?` collapsed "spawn failed" and "ran and said
nothing" into one `None`. Lesson: when two opposite conditions share a return
value, the debugging cost lands years later on someone else. The gap entry was
replaced, not appended to.
